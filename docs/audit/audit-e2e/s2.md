### S2-01 · The fill-event ring overwrites unmirrored fills and nothing on either side can detect the gap

- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P0
- **Tag:** [reachable-now]
- **Class:** S2-C8
- **Pairs with:** `S2-X01` — the consumer half of this same defect, routed to S4.
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:206-222`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:191-205`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/mirror_fills.rs:125-134`, `docs/02-orderbook-and-pda-storage.md:192-195`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:137-141`

**What is wrong.** `push_fill_event` is a true ring: at capacity it overwrites the
oldest entry, advances `fill_event_head`, pins `fill_event_count` at `max_fill_events`,
and returns `Ok(())` (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:212-221`). The header records no drop counter and
no "oldest surviving sequence", so the account itself carries no trace that an entry was
destroyed. On the consuming side `mirror_fills` walks the ring from `head`, skips
`fill.sequence <= last_mirrored`, and then sets
`log.header.last_mirrored_sequence = new_max_seq` (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/mirror_fills.rs:125-152`) — it never
checks that the oldest surviving sequence is `last_mirrored + 1`, so a hole in the
sequence space is stepped straight over and the cursor is advanced past it permanently.
The written invariant this violates is `docs/02-orderbook-and-pda-storage.md:192-195`:
*"The ring must hold enough history that no live fill is overwritten before it's been
mirrored to L1."* The in-code safety argument at `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:200-205` states its own
precondition — *"the keeper runs continuously, so already-mirrored old fills are the only
thing ever overwritten"* — and §6 K1 establishes that precondition is false: the keepers
have been down since 2026-08-05. This is not a re-report of K1; it is the residual gap
in a mitigation whose stated premise K1 removes.

A lost fill is destroyed money, not just a missing record. The maker's ER margin is
debited unconditionally on their next interaction — `reconcile_credit` computes
`drained = committed - actual_committed` and does `credit.credit -= drained`
(`programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:137-141`) purely from the slot's shrunken `margin_reserved`, with no
reference to whether the corresponding `FillEvent` ever reached L1. The matching credit
to `Position.collateral` happens only in settlement, off the `FillEvent`. Overwrite the
event and the debit still happens; the credit never does.

**Proof.** Live read of the deployed book and market
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/onchain-read.txt`, ER slot
546316629 / L1 slot 486305361, 2026-08-21T19:29:01Z) — the ring is **at capacity right
now**, in the overwrite regime, with `head == tail`:

```
   max_fill_events              4096
   fill_event_head              3229
   fill_event_tail              3229
   fill_event_count             4096
   next_fill_sequence           44190
   last_settled_sequence        44173     (Market, L1)
-- ring holds sequences 40094 .. 44189; 16 fill(s) produced but not settled
-- 4079 already-settled entries stand between the write head and the oldest unsettled fill
```

And the mechanism, isolated in `docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`
(`s2_01_fill_ring_overwrites_without_signalling`):

```
ring at capacity      : [1, 2, 3, 4] count=4 head=0 tail=0
after two more pushes : [5, 6, 3, 4] count=4 head=2 tail=2
LOST: sequences 1,2 are unrecoverable; header exposes only count=4 head=2 tail=2
```

Two pushes past capacity destroyed sequences 1 and 2. Both `push_fill_event` calls
returned `Ok`; `fill_event_count` did not move.

**Blast radius.** Every maker and taker whose fill is overwritten before
`mirror_fills` reaches it. Each lost `FillEvent` strands its `filled_margin` — the
maker's ER credit is debited and no `Position.collateral` is ever created for it, and the
taker's ER credit was already debited inside `place_order` — so the quote-asset value is
destroyed with no on-chain record of where it went. Precondition on the deployed book
today: 4,080 further fills while `mirror_fills` stays dead, at which point sequence 44174
is overwritten. That is not a hypothetical volume — the book has produced 44,189 fills
already, and two funded wallets crossing in the ER (≈10 ms per transaction, sponsored)
reach 4,080 in minutes. It is also usable as a targeted attack: trade against a victim,
then flood the ring to bury the victim's unsettled fill. On mainnet the loss is
denominated in real collateral.

**Suggested remediation.** Make the ring lossless the way `FillLogView::push` already is
(`programs/slipstream/src/state/fill_log.rs:83-97` refuses rather than overwrites, for
exactly this reason) — or, if trading must not stall, add a `dropped_fill_count` /
`oldest_surviving_sequence` pair to `OrderBookHeader`'s spare padding and have
`mirror_fills` fail loudly when `oldest_surviving_sequence > last_mirrored_sequence + 1`
instead of advancing across the hole.

### S2-02 · The matching engine never reads `expiry_ts`; an expired order stays fillable indefinitely

- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P1
- **Tag:** [reachable-now]
- **Class:** S2-C9
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:363-472`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:405-419`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:88`, `keepers/src/expiry-keeper.ts:9`, `tests/unit/src/test_cancel_order_expiry_regressions.rs:6-10`

**What is wrong.** `match_order` — the whole of `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:363-472` — never reads
`OrderSlot::expiry_ts`. The maker snapshot it takes is `(remaining_size, owner, side)`
only (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:405-408`), and the fill proceeds on price alone. The only on-chain
place `expiry_ts` is consulted is `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:88`, which merely *permits* a
permissionless cancel; it requires someone to send a transaction. Enforcement therefore
lives entirely in `keepers/src/expiry-keeper.ts:9` ("scans the OrderBook every
`SCAN_INTERVAL_MS` and submits `cancel_order` for each slot whose `expiry_ts > 0 &&
expiry_ts <= now`"). The invariant is written down in the repo's own regression-test
header, `tests/unit/src/test_cancel_order_expiry_regressions.rs:6-10`, which describes
the pre-fix state as "`expiry_ts` was entirely unenforced on-chain" and frames the
permissionless-cancel bypass as the fix. It is only half a fix: the bypass made expiry
*cleanable*, not *unmatchable*, and the cleaner is a process that §6 K1 establishes has
been dead for 17 days.

**Proof.** A grep over the whole program showing the two consultation sites and their
kind — nothing in the matching path:

```
$ grep -rn "expiry_ts" programs/slipstream/src
programs/slipstream/src/state/order_slot.rs:34:    pub expiry_ts: i64,
programs/slipstream/src/state/order_slot.rs:61:        expiry_ts: i64,
programs/slipstream/src/state/order_slot.rs:75:        self.expiry_ts = expiry_ts;
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:34:///   expiry_ts:        i64   (0 = never)
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:85:    let expiry_ts = i64::from_le_bytes(data[18..26].try_into().unwrap());
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:281:            expiry_ts,
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:21:///  order's `expiry_ts` has passed.
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:25:/// own `expiry_ts` was already consented to being cancelled at ANY time after
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:88:        let expired = slot.expiry_ts > 0 && now >= slot.expiry_ts;
```

`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:85` and `:281` are write-only (parse and store); `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:88` is
the cancel bypass. And the behaviour end to end, against the real compiled program
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`,
`s2_02_expired_resting_order_is_still_matched`) — a resting ask that expired an hour
before the clock is matched in full:

```
place_order against an order expired at 1699996400 (clock 1700000000): Success
after: fill_event_count=1 ask_level_count=0 active_order_count=0 fill.qty=100000000 fill.price=150000000
```

**Blast radius.** Any maker who used `expiry_ts` as a risk control. Their quote stays
executable at its stale price for as long as nobody cancels it, so the loss is the
adverse-selection cost of being picked off at a price the maker had explicitly stopped
standing behind — unbounded in a gapping market, and exactly the cost `expiry_ts` exists
to cap. `[reachable-now]` because the sole enforcer is a dead keeper: an order placed
with an expiry today would never be cancelled. Honest limit on the evidence: the 12
orders currently resting on devnet all carry `expiry_ts == 0`
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/onchain-read.txt`, "resting orders already past their
own expiry_ts: 0"), so no live
order is in the harmed state at this instant — the exposure is to the next one placed.

**Suggested remediation.** Have `match_order` skip (and free) a maker slot whose
`expiry_ts > 0 && now >= expiry_ts` before filling against it, rather than trusting an
off-chain scanner to have removed it. That also makes the keeper a pure
compute-optimisation instead of a correctness dependency.

### S2-03 · Session-key expiry is decided by a clock the ER sequencer controls

- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P1
- **Tag:** [mainnet-only]
- **Class:** S2-C11
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:148-152`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:50-56`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:110-117`, `docs/06-session-keys.md:42`, `docs/03-ephemeral-rollups-and-delegation.md:120`

**What is wrong.** Both instructions this slice owns take their only notion of time from
`Clock::get()` evaluated *inside the ephemeral rollup* (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:148`,
`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:50`), and hand it to `is_authorized_signer`, whose session test is
exactly `now < self.session_expiry` (`programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:114-116`). The sequencer supplies
that sysvar. Two properties therefore do not survive a hostile sequencer:

- **Session-key expiry.** `docs/06-session-keys.md:42` states "Once `now >=
  session_expiry`, the key is automatically rejected", and `:100` lists "Act after
  expiry — **No**". Both are true only for an honest clock. Rewinding it revives every
  session key the ER has ever seen a signature from, and the ER has seen all of them.
- **Order permanence.** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:88` gates the permissionless cancel on the same
  clock, so advancing it lets any signer cancel every resting order that carries a
  non-zero `expiry_ts`.

This is not covered by the documented ER trust posture.
`docs/03-ephemeral-rollups-and-delegation.md:120` states the guarantee as "because funds
never leave L1, a buggy or malicious ER can at worst corrupt order *ordering* — it can
never move a single token of anyone's money", and `:110-118` classes the OrderBook as
"non-financial". Clock control is not order-ordering: it reaches the authorization gate
that decides *who may bind the owner's margin*, and the orders it lets through become
FillEvents that settlement turns into L1 `Position.collateral` movements. The documented
mitigation does not hold at this seam.

**Proof.** The authorization gate is a pure function of the supplied clock. Same
instruction, same accounts, same dead key — only the sysvar differs
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`,
`s2_05_rewinding_the_er_clock_revives_an_expired_session_key`; Mollusk stands in for the
sequencer by writing the same sysvar the ER controls):

```
clock = 1700000000 (honest, expiry 1699996400): Failure(Custom(257))
clock = 1699992800 (rewound by the sequencer):        Success
  -> order rested: active_order_count=1 owner=the CREDIT OWNER
```

`Custom(257)` is `SlipstreamError::InvalidAuthority`. And the grep confirming there is no
second time source and no other consumer of the session gate:

```
$ grep -rn "is_authorized_signer" programs/slipstream/src
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:150:        if !credit.is_authorized_signer(signer.key(), now) {
programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:56:        is_owner_or_session = credit.is_authorized_signer(signer.key(), now);
programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:110:    pub fn is_authorized_signer(&self, signer: &Pubkey, now: i64) -> bool {
```

**Blast radius.** Bounded by one `TradingCredit`'s `credit` per user per market, which is
the scope the session-key design promises — but the promise that the scope *ends* at
`session_expiry` is void. Harmed party: any user who ever authorized a session key and
believes it is now dead. `[mainnet-only]`: on devnet the ER is operated by MagicBlock and
`docs/03-ephemeral-rollups-and-delegation.md:158-162` declares operator trust an accepted
devnet condition, so there is no adversary today; on a real-money deployment with a
sequencer that is not the protocol's friend, this is the difference between a revocable
and an irrevocable delegation.

**Suggested remediation.** Do not let ER-local time terminate a delegation. Stamp
`session_expiry` against an L1-anchored value — e.g. record the L1 slot at
`authorize_session` and bound the session in slots the ER cannot fabricate — or require
the owner's own signature for any action after a session's nominal expiry so a rewound
clock buys nothing.

### S2-04 · The `orders_per_user` gate narrows a `u16` counter to `u8` before comparing it

- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P2
- **Class:** S2-C9
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:211`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:33`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:126-142`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:10`

**What is wrong.** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:211` reads
`if (credit.active_orders as u8) >= orders_per_user`. `active_orders` is a `u16`
(`programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:33`) and is not a value the gate itself keeps small: two lines
earlier, `reconcile_credit` *overwrites* it with a full scan of the caller's active slots
in the book (`programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:126-142`), a count bounded only by `max_order_slots`
(2048), not by `orders_per_user` (20, `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:10`). The cast silently discards the
high byte, so the cap is a function of `active_orders mod 256` — it fires at 255 owned
slots and does not fire at 256. No invariant tying `active_orders` to `< 256` is written
down anywhere; the bound is purely emergent from the gate it is supposed to feed, which
is the circularity that makes this a missing defence rather than a live exploit.

**Proof.** The truncation, demonstrated against the real compiled program by seeding the
book to either side of the wrap (`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`,
`s2_04_orders_per_user_gate_truncates_at_256`):

```
owner already holds 511 active slots (orders_per_user = 20, 511 as u8 = 255): Failure(Custom(291))  CU = 32561
owner already holds 512 active slots (orders_per_user = 20, 512 as u8 = 0): Success  CU = 33127
  -> order accepted; owner now holds 513 active slots, 25x the declared cap of 20
```

`Custom(291)` is `SlipstreamError::MaxOrdersPerUser`. Stated plainly: reaching 256 owned
slots through the program's own front door requires the gate to have already been
bypassed 256 times, so this is **not** reachable on its own today — the repro seeds the
state directly. It is a second line of defence that is not there.

**Blast radius.** None today. If any future path can raise a user's slot count without
passing this gate — a wider `orders_per_user`, a bulk-place instruction, a matching bug
that leaks slots, or a book initialized with a larger `max_order_slots` — one account
silently occupies the shared 2048-slot pool and locks every other trader out of the book
with `OrderBookFull`.

**Suggested remediation.** Compare in the wider type:
`if credit.active_orders >= orders_per_user as u16`. One-line change, no layout impact.

### S2-05 · `taker_margin == filled_margin` is asserted in two comments and enforced in neither

- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P3
- **Class:** S2-C4
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:423-428`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_slot.rs:94-105`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/settle_trades.rs:223`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/initialize_market.rs:85`

**What is wrong.** Two different formulas are treated as producing the same number. On
the ER side, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:426-428` debits the taker
`compute_initial_margin(compute_notional(fill_qty, level_price), leverage)`, and comments
the step "same-leverage MVP => taker_margin == filled_margin" (`:425`). On the L1 side,
`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/settle_trades.rs:223` credits the **taker's** `Position.collateral` with
`fill.filled_margin` — the *maker*-derived number, produced by
`OrderSlot::drain_margin_for_fill`'s floored proportional share
(`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_slot.rs:97-101`) — and repeats the same claim in its own comment. Nothing checks
the equality; it holds only when every floor in both chains happens to be exact. It is
exact for the deployed market (`tick_size` 1,000 · `lot_size` 1e8 · `max_leverage` 20
makes every notional a multiple of 100 and 100/20 = 5), and `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/initialize_market.rs:85`
accepts any `max_leverage` in `1..=255`, so a market one instruction away breaks it.

**Proof.** A model of the two cited code paths, transcribed line for line, run over a
maker slot swept by three takers (`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`,
`s2_03_taker_debit_can_differ_from_credited_filled_margin`; `max_leverage = 3`,
`price = 149_000_000`, three 0.1-SOL lots):

```
fill qty=100000000: taker debited 4966666, taker credited 4966666
fill qty=100000000: taker debited 4966666, taker credited 4966667
fill qty=100000000: taker debited 4966666, taker credited 4966667
maker margin drained  : 14900000 (credited to maker positions: 14900000)
taker debited (ER)    : 14899998
taker credited (L1)   : 14900000
delta minted from air : 2
```

The maker side conserves exactly. The taker side does not: 2 quote atoms of
`Position.collateral` exist that nobody's `TradingCredit` paid for.

**Blast radius.** Zero on the deployed market — the divisions are exact there, which is
why nothing has surfaced it. On a market with a `max_leverage` that does not divide the
per-lot notional, each fill mints or destroys up to `max_leverage - 1` quote atoms
(≤ 2.5e-4 USDC), against the whole vault's backing. The real cost is that the invariant
is load-bearing for settlement's value conservation and exists only as prose in two
files that can drift apart.

**Suggested remediation.** Stop computing the taker's margin twice. Stamp the taker's
debit on the `FillEvent` alongside `filled_margin` (there are 3 spare bytes plus room in
the 104-byte record) and have `settle_trades` credit each side the number that side
actually paid; or assert the equality on the ER side and fail the fill if it does not
hold.

### S2-X01 · `mirror_fills` advances `last_mirrored_sequence` across a gap it never checks for

- **Routed to:** S4
- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P0
- **Tag:** [reachable-now]
- **Class:** S4-C1
- **Pairs with:** `S2-01` — the producer half of this same defect, filed in S2.
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/mirror_fills.rs:118-152`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/mirror_fills.rs:104`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:206-222`

**What is wrong.** This is the consumer half of `S2-01` and it lives in S4's file set. The
scan starts at the ring's `head`, skips anything with `sequence <= last_mirrored`, and
finishes with `log.header.last_mirrored_sequence = new_max_seq`
(`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/mirror_fills.rs:125-152`). There is no comparison between `last_mirrored + 1` and the
oldest sequence actually present in the ring, so if `push_fill_event` overwrote the
entries in between, the cursor jumps the hole and those fills are never mirrored, never
settled, and never retried. The care taken one branch away is the tell: the code
deliberately does *not* advance the cursor when `log.push` refuses (`:127-134`), because
that entry is recoverable — but an entry the OrderBook ring already destroyed gets no
such treatment because nothing looks for it.

**Proof.** See `S2-01` — same live account state
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/onchain-read.txt`: ring at capacity, `head == tail ==
3229`, `next_fill_sequence` 44190, `last_settled_sequence` 44173) and the same repro
(`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs`,
`s2_01_fill_ring_overwrites_without_signalling`), which shows the producing side
destroying sequences with no signal for this consumer to read.

**Blast radius.** Identical to `S2-01`: silently unsettled fills, quote value debited on
the ER with no L1 counterpart. Routed because the fix on this side —
a `last_mirrored + 1` continuity check that errors instead of advancing — belongs to S4.

**Suggested remediation.** Before appending, require the ring's oldest surviving
sequence to be `<= last_mirrored_sequence + 1`; otherwise return an error naming the gap
so the operator learns about the loss instead of inheriting a silently-advanced cursor.

### S2-X02 · `place_order` / `cancel_order` never pin the TradingCredit account's owner or PDA

- **Routed to:** S1
- **Status:** CONFIRMED
- **Severity:** P2
- **Class:** S1-C2
- **Evidence:** `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:112-122`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:146-157`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:53-67`, `programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:51-60`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/initialize_trading_credit.rs:40`

**What is wrong.** Both instructions pin every other account they read: `global_state` by
owner and PDA (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:72-78`), `market` by owner and PDA (`:112-122`, with a
comment explaining exactly why), `order_book` by PDA (`:179-185`, `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:60-66`).
`trading_credit_acc` is pinned by neither. `TradingCredit::from_account_info`
(`programs/slipstream/src/state/trading_credit.rs:51-60`) validates length and discriminator only — not
`account.owner() == program_id` — and neither caller derives
`[SEED_CREDIT, owner, market_index]`, the seeds `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/initialize_trading_credit.rs:40` uses.
The `owner` field inside the account is therefore self-asserted rather than proven by its

## Cleared

Negative results. Every threat class in `docs/checks/audit-e2e/classes.tsv` for S2,
including those that produced a finding.

- **S2-C1** — Zero-copy slice safety. `OrderBookHeader::compute_account_size`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:43-54`) and the four `from_raw_parts_mut`
  casts (`:111-148`) are guarded by `data.len() < expected_size` computed from the
  header's *own* capacities (`:92-100`), so a partially grown account cannot be parsed at
  all. Element strides are 88 / 16 / 104 / 2 bytes and the header is 48, so every section
  offset stays 8-byte aligned for any capacity triple. Verified against the real
  `grow_orderbook` chunk boundary: 47, 48, 10 240 and `full-1` bytes are all rejected,
  only 626 736 is accepted (`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/output.txt`,
  `s2_negative_partially_grown_book_is_rejected`). The one soft spot is that
  `bytemuck::from_bytes` at `:87` would panic rather than error on a misaligned buffer;
  Solana's aligned loader guarantees 8-byte alignment, so this is not filed.
- **S2-C2** — Free-list integrity. Sound. `alloc_slot`/`free_slot`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:161-179`) are the only mutators and
  `free_slot` clears the slot, so a second `cancel_order` on the same id fails at
  `find_order_by_id` (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:149-156`)
  before reaching a double free. Checked three ways: a walk of the **live** free list
  from `free_list_head = 37` returns exactly 2 036 entries with no cycle and no
  out-of-range index, matching the header, and free ∪ active covers all 2 048 slots with
  empty intersection (`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/onchain-read.txt`); the same walk
  plus `active + free == max_order_slots` is asserted after every step of a
  place/cancel/fill script driven through the compiled program
  (`docs/audit/audit-e2e/repro/s2/src/lib.rs:s2_negative_book_invariants_hold_under_place_cancel_fill`);
  and `overflow-checks = true` now sits at the workspace root (`Cargo.toml:14`), so an
  `active_order_count` underflow would abort rather than wrap.
- **S2-C3** — Index bounds. Sound *for a book only the deployed program has written*, and
  for slot indices only by way of a header field: `alloc_slot`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:165-169`) indexes
  `free_list[free_list_head as usize]` and returns `free_list_head` itself as the slot
  index, with no range test, so slot-index safety reduces to `free_list_head <
  max_order_slots` — an invariant every L1 write path preserves and nothing re-checks on
  load (`OrderBookView::from_account_data` bounds only the account length,
  `programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:92-100`). For ER-authored bytes that
  field is sequencer-chosen, so the bound is assumed there, not verified; see
  the hostile-sequencer entry below and `S5-C2`. The remaining indices are structurally
  bounded: the `0..max_order_slots` cancel scan
  (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:150-155`); level indices come
  from `find_bid_level`/`find_ask_level`, which search only `..count`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:265-280`); the fill index is `(tail + 1) % max` (`:213`). `SENTINEL`
  is tested at every list terminus before it is used as an index —
  `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:400-402` and `:447-464`,
  `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/cancel_order.rs:102-107`. The invariant checker in the repro additionally walks every
  level's intrusive FIFO and asserts each link is in range, active, at the level's price,
  and back-pointed correctly, after every mutation.
- **S2-C4** — Arithmetic overflow. One P3 filed, `S2-05`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:423-428`). The counters are
  otherwise safe: `active_order_count` / `bid_level_count` / `ask_level_count` are
  bounded by `max_order_slots` and `max_price_levels_per_side`, `fill_event_count` is
  clamped at `max` (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:214-220`), and notional
  and margin both go through `u128` with `checked_mul` and a `u64::MAX` guard
  (`programs/slipstream/src/math/fixed_point.rs:53-70`). `next_order_id` and
  `next_fill_sequence` are bare `+= 1` (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:345`, `:352`), which is a real
  wrap at `u64::MAX` — 44 190 fills after ~3 months, so ~10^14 years away; not filed.
  With `overflow-checks = true` at `Cargo.toml:14` every one of these aborts rather than
  wraps if the reasoning above is ever wrong.
- **S2-C5** — Price-time priority. Sound. Price priority: the ladders are kept strictly
  sorted by the insert/remove shift pair
  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/order_book.rs:283-340`) and matching always reads index

  (`programs/slipstream/src/state/price_level.rs:35-38`,
  `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:288-291`) and matching always takes `head_slot`

- **S2-C6** — Self-trade. Sound. `match_order` compares the maker's slot `owner` against

- **S2-C11** — ER-side execution under a hostile sequencer. Covered by **S2-03** (P1,
  `programs/slipstream/src/instructions/place_order.rs:148-152`). Stating the rest of the
  survival list explicitly, per the spec. Six properties are **assumed, not verified** —
  price-level sortedness, intra-level FIFO order, free-list integrity, index bounds,
  self-trade rejection and the POST_ONLY cross test. Each is a pure function of the
  account bytes, but the sequencer authors those bytes: the OrderBook is delegated by
  the same mechanism that delegates `TradingCredit`
  (`programs/slipstream/src/instructions/delegate_orderbook.rs:187-193`), and `S5-01` /

  cancel gate, both `Clock::get()`-derived — filed as `S2-03`; (c) `FillEvent` contents,
  which are ER-authored and become L1 money at settlement — that is S4-C8/S5-C2 territory
  and is left to those slices rather than re-filed here.
