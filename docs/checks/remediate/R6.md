# Frozen check — R6 `funding-clamp`

**Run:** `remediate` · **Slice:** `funding-clamp` · **Issue:** `docs/runs/remediate/issues/06-funding-clamp.md`
**Spec:** `docs/spec/remediate.md` — Decision 7

## Purpose

`compute_funding` is permissionless and multiplies an unclamped premium by an unclamped catch-up
interval count. Replaying live devnet state, one call applies **−908.15 % of notional**: 48
intervals × an 18.93 % premium. This slice puts both bounds in the math module, where a
permissionless instruction can still be safe.

## Fix contract

- Item **1 or 2** failing is an environment problem, not a builder defect — see R1's contract.
- A failure of **4** means one or both bounds are missing, are the wrong sign, or are set so loose
  they do not bound the live replay. Fix `programs/slipstream/src/math/funding.rs` — never the test.
  The **values** are policy (intake ruling 5: ±0.5 % per interval, 3 intervals) and may be changed by
  a ruling; the **shape** — a clamped premium times a capped catch-up — is the safety property and
  may not.
- A failure of **5** means the clamp swallowed ordinary funding: a zero-premium interval must still
  return exactly `INTEREST_RATE_PER_INTERVAL` (10^14). A clamp tighter than normal funding is a
  different defect, not a stronger fix.
- A failure of **6** means the two existing funding tests broke. `test_compute_funding_regressions`
  pins premium at exactly zero (`:109`), which is why it never caught `S3-01`; it must still pass
  unmodified, and it must not be edited to accommodate the clamp.
- A failure of **7** means the constants are not `pub`, so the test asserts against a hard-coded copy
  and silently stops tracking the real bound. Export them from `math::funding`.
- A failure of **8** is a lint regression introduced by this slice.

Duration hint: item 1 is a full SBF rebuild, roughly 1–3 minutes cold. This slice's own test is pure
math and needs no SBF artifact, but item 2 does.

## What each item asserts, and why it can fail

1. The SBF artifact exists.
2. **No regression.** All 123 pre-existing tests still pass.
3. The clamp test exists and passes. The `match` is mandatory: a filter matching zero tests exits 0.
4. **The live replay is bounded.** `compute_funding_rate(74_114_120, 91_317_865)` — the exact mark
   and index read from devnet — returns a magnitude at most `MAX_FUNDING_RATE_PER_INTERVAL`, and
   `MAX_FUNDING_RATE_PER_INTERVAL * MAX_CATCHUP_INTERVALS` is at most 10 % of `FUNDING_SCALE`. That
   composed bound is the safety property: it is what makes one permissionless call harmless.
   Verified to fail against the unmodified program — with a **compile error**, `E0432: no
   MAX_FUNDING_RATE_PER_INTERVAL in math::funding`, because neither constant exists.
5. A zero-premium interval still returns exactly 10^14, so the clamp does not swallow normal funding.
6. Both existing funding tests pass unmodified.
7. `MAX_FUNDING_RATE_PER_INTERVAL` is a `pub const` in the math module, not a literal inside
   `compute_funding_rate`.
8. `MAX_CATCHUP_INTERVALS` likewise.
9. The interval cap is actually applied in the instruction, not merely declared in the math module.
   Scoped to one file, so no self-match and no repo-name collision.
10. No new lint.

## Not graded by the runner — for the closing review

- **The aggregate is not bounded by this fix, and that is deliberate.** `compute_funding.rs:45`
  advances `last_funding_ts` by `intervals * interval_secs`, not to `now` — "any partial remainder
  stays owed". With `intervals` capped, the live 48-interval backlog is **deferred**, paid down over
  ~16 further permissionless calls, roughly ±24 % of notional in aggregate. The per-call bound holds;
  the aggregate does not. Open decision 8 chooses deferred vs forgiven; default **forgive**
  (`last_funding_ts = now` when `intervals > MAX_CATCHUP_INTERVALS`). Whichever the orchestrator
  rules, the graded items are unchanged.
- **No existing position's settlement math changes.** The clamp bounds only future increments of
  `cumulative_funding_index`; each position settles against the delta since its own snapshot. The
  review should confirm no already-accrued value is rewritten.
- `crank_twap`'s missing signer (`S5-03`, P1) is adjacent and deliberately out of scope. The clamp is
  the root-cause fix and does not depend on it.
